\subsection{Statistical Significance}
\label{sec:stats}

Because all four models were tested on the identical 128 items (Speaker~0,
FS=0), model-vs-model comparisons can be evaluated with McNemar's exact test
on matched pairs rather than an independent-samples test. Every
Gemini-vs-GPT comparison is significant (all $p<.001$), while the two GPT
models do not differ significantly from each other ($p=.25$); see
Table~\ref{tab:mcnemar}. For the few-shot manipulation, each FS=2 item is
evaluated across 3--4 cross-validation folds, so trials are not independent;
we therefore fit a GEE logistic regression with cluster-robust standard
errors (item as cluster) rather than treating fold-repeated trials as
independent observations. No model shows a statistically reliable few-shot
trend on the inference task (all $p>.2$; Table~\ref{tab:fewshot}).
